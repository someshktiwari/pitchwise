# Indian Premier League (IPL)

The Indian Premier League is a franchise-based Twenty20 cricket league in India, and the most commercially successful domestic cricket competition in the world.

## IPL Overview

- The IPL was founded in **2008**, the IPL was launched partly in response to the excitement generated by India's win at the inaugural 2007 T20 World Cup.
- Consists of franchise teams representing Indian cities, with squads built through a player auction system that allows teams to bid for both domestic and international players.
- Regarded as the wealthiest cricket league in the world, with broadcasting and sponsorship rights valued in the billions of dollars, and has become a major driver of the global T20 economy.

## Format

- A round-robin group stage followed by playoffs (Qualifiers and an Eliminator) leading to a final.
- Typically runs for around two months, usually between March and May.
- Player auctions and mid-season trades allow franchises to reshape their squads between (and occasionally during) seasons.

## IPL Notable Franchises

- **Mumbai Indians** is one of the most successful franchises in IPL history, with a roster that has featured Jasprit Bumrah since 2013.
- **Royal Challengers Bengaluru** has been Virat Kohli's IPL franchise since the league's inception in 2008, one of the most popular teams despite a long wait for their first title.

## Significance

The IPL's success has directly inspired the creation of similar T20 franchise leagues around the cricketing world, including the Big Bash League (Australia), The Hundred (England), and various others, fundamentally reshaping the economics and calendar of the modern game.
