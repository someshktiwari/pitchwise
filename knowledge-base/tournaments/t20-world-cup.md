# ICC Men's T20 World Cup

The ICC Men's T20 World Cup is the premier international championship of Twenty20 International (T20I) cricket.

## T20 World Cup Overview

- The T20 World Cup was first held in **2007** in South Africa, won by India in a dramatic final against Pakistan.
- Held biennially (every 2 years) since 2022; previously held at less regular intervals.
- Features a larger pool of participating nations than the ODI World Cup, reflecting T20 cricket's lower barrier to competitiveness for associate nations.

## Format

- Group stage followed by a Super 8 or Super 12 stage (format has varied by edition), then semi-finals and a final.
- Given the shorter format, tournaments are typically completed within about 3-4 weeks.

## T20 World Cup Notable Editions

- **2007 T20 World Cup** (South Africa) — the inaugural edition, won by India, who defeated Pakistan in a nail-biting final. This victory is widely credited with helping spark the launch of the Indian Premier League the following year.
- **2024 T20 World Cup** (USA and West Indies) — India won their second T20 World Cup title, defeating South Africa by 7 runs in the final, with Virat Kohli named Player of the Match. Kohli, Rohit Sharma, and Ravindra Jadeja all announced their T20I retirements immediately after the win.
- **2026 T20 World Cup** (India) — India successfully defended their title, with Jasprit Bumrah named Player of the Match in the final held at the Narendra Modi Stadium, Ahmedabad.

## Significance

The tournament has become one of the most commercially valuable events in world cricket, reflecting the broader global rise of T20 cricket since the mid-2000s.
