# Kane Williamson

**Full name:** Kane Stuart Williamson
**Born:** 8 August 1990, Tauranga, New Zealand
**Role:** Top-order batter
**Batting style:** Right-handed
**Bowling style:** Right-arm off-break
**National side:** New Zealand (2010–present)
**Domestic team:** Northern Districts

## Career Overview

Kane Williamson is regarded as one of the "Fab Four" modern batting greats alongside Virat Kohli, Steve Smith, and Joe Root. Known for his calm temperament and technical orthodoxy, he made his Test and ODI debuts against India in 2010 and has captained New Zealand across formats for much of his career.

## Kane Williamson's Notable Records

- Kane Williamson became the fastest New Zealand batter, and fifth-fastest overall, to reach 3,000 ODI runs
- One of only a handful of players to score a century on Test debut
- Reached 9,000 Test runs faster (in fewer matches) than all but seven other players in history

## Kane Williamson's Key Achievements

- Kane Williamson was Player of the Tournament at the 2019 Cricket World Cup, where he carried New Zealand to the final
- Captained New Zealand to the final of both the 2019 and 2023 Cricket World Cups, and won the inaugural ICC World Test Championship in 2021
- Widely praised for his sportsmanship, including offering to share the 2019 World Cup trophy with England after the controversial boundary-count tiebreaker
- Known as one of the most technically correct and consistent batters of his generation across all three formats

## Kane Williamson's Franchise Career and Standing

Kane Williamson has played franchise cricket worldwide, including stints with Sunrisers Hyderabad and Gujarat Titans in the IPL, Yorkshire in county cricket, and various T20 leagues. Regarded as a calming, senior presence in the New Zealand dressing room even after stepping back from full-time captaincy duties.
