# Jasprit Bumrah

**Full name:** Jasprit Jasbirsingh Bumrah
**Born:** 6 December 1993, Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India
**Role:** Bowler
**Batting style:** Right-handed
**Bowling style:** Right-arm fast
**National side:** India (2016–present)
**Domestic teams:** Gujarat, Mumbai Indians (IPL, since 2013)

## Career Overview

Jasprit Bumrah is widely considered one of the finest fast bowlers of his generation, known for his unorthodox action and exceptional death-bowling skill. He made his T20I and ODI debuts in January 2016 against Australia, and his Test debut on 5 January 2018 against South Africa, taking a five-wicket haul in only his third Test.

He has served as Test vice-captain since December 2023 and has captained India in Tests in the absence of the regular captain. He is the first bowler to be ranked No. 1 in all three ICC formats simultaneously.

## Jasprit Bumrah's Career Statistics (as of early 2026)

- **Tests:** Jasprit Bumrah has played 52 matches, taking 234 wickets, average 19.79, best figures 6/27
- **ODIs:** 89 matches, 149 wickets, average 23.55, best figures 6/19
- **T20Is:** 95 matches, 121 wickets, average 18.08, best figures 4/15

## Jasprit Bumrah's Key Achievements

- Jasprit Bumrah was named Player of the Match in the 2026 T20 World Cup final
- Leading wicket-taker for India in the 2026 T20 World Cup (part of India's title-winning campaign)
- Won the T20 World Cup twice with India (2024 and 2026)
- ICC Men's Test Cricketer of the Year, 2024
- Named in the ICC Men's Test Team of the Year (2018, 2024), ODI Team of the Year (2017, 2018), and T20I Team of the Year
- Holds the best career bowling average among Indian bowlers with 50+ ODI wickets

## Jasprit Bumrah's Personal Life

Jasprit Bumrah married sports anchor Sanjana Ganesan in 2021.
