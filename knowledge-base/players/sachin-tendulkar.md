# Sachin Tendulkar

**Full name:** Sachin Ramesh Tendulkar
**Born:** 24 April 1973, Mumbai, India
**Role:** Top-order batter
**Batting style:** Right-handed
**Bowling style:** Right-arm medium, occasional leg-spin
**National side:** India (1989–2013, retired)
**Domestic teams:** Mumbai, Mumbai Indians (IPL)

## Career Overview

Sachin Tendulkar is widely regarded as one of the greatest batters in the history of cricket. He made his international debut at age 16 in 1989 against Pakistan and retired in November 2013 after a 24-year international career, playing his 200th and final Test at his home ground, Wankhede Stadium, Mumbai.

## Sachin Tendulkar's Career Statistics (career totals, retired 2013)

- **Tests:** Sachin Tendulkar scored 15,921 runs in 200 matches, average 53.78, 51 centuries — the most Test runs and centuries by any player in history
- **ODIs:** 18,426 runs in 463 matches, average 44.83, 49 centuries — the first player to score a double century in ODI cricket (200* vs South Africa, 2010)
- **Total international centuries:** 100 — the first and only player to reach this milestone

## Sachin Tendulkar's Key Achievements

- Sachin Tendulkar is the only player to score 100 international centuries
- Leading run-scorer in both Test and ODI cricket
- Part of India's 2011 Cricket World Cup-winning squad
- Played the most Test matches (200) of any player in history
- Received the Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian award, in 2014

## Legacy

Tendulkar's ODI record of 49 centuries stood as the benchmark for over a decade until Virat Kohli surpassed it in 2023. His combination of longevity, consistency, and technical mastery across formats set the standard that subsequent generations of Indian batters have been measured against.
