# Virat Kohli

**Full name:** Virat Kohli
**Born:** 5 November 1988, New Delhi, India
**Role:** Top-order batter
**Batting style:** Right-handed
**Bowling style:** Right-arm medium
**National side:** India (2008–present)
**Domestic teams:** Delhi, Royal Challengers Bengaluru (IPL, since 2008)

## Career Overview

Virat Kohli is widely regarded as one of the greatest batters in modern cricket history and one of the most successful ODI chasers the game has seen. He made his ODI debut on 18 August 2008 against Sri Lanka, his Test debut on 20 June 2011 against West Indies, and his T20I debut on 12 June 2010 against Zimbabwe.

He retired from Test cricket in 2025 and from T20I cricket in 2024, remaining active in ODI cricket.

## Virat Kohli's Career Statistics (as of mid-2026)

- **Tests:** Virat Kohli has scored 9,230 runs in 123 matches, average 46.85, 30 centuries (retired May 2025)
- **ODIs:** close to 14,800 runs, average near 58, a world-record 54 centuries (surpassing Sachin Tendulkar's 49 during the 2023 World Cup), still active
- **T20Is:** 4,188 runs, average 48.69, one century (retired June 2024)
- **Highest scores by format:** 254* (Tests, vs South Africa, Pune 2019), 183 (ODIs, vs Pakistan, 2012 Asia Cup, Mirpur), 122* (T20Is, vs Afghanistan, 2022)
- **Total international centuries:** 85 — second only to Sachin Tendulkar's 100

## Virat Kohli's Key Achievements

- Virat Kohli holds the world record for most ODI centuries (54)
- Player of the Match in the 2024 T20 World Cup final, scoring 76 off 59 balls
- Former captain of the Indian Test, ODI, and T20I teams
- Scored Test centuries in and against every Test-playing nation except Bangladesh
- Known as the "Chase Master" for his exceptional record batting second in ODI run chases

## Personal

Married to actor Anushka Sharma since 2017. Known for his intense fitness regimen, which has influenced a generation of Indian cricketers' approach to conditioning.
